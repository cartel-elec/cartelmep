About CARTEL MULTI-ENGINEERING LTD

A professional electrical installation and engineering company dedicated to safe, reliable and high-quality solutions across Rwanda.

Who We Are

CARTEL MULTI-ENGINEERING LTD is an electrical installation and engineering company based in Rwanda. We provide design, installation, maintenance and support services for residential, commercial, industrial and institutional clients.

Our work covers the full lifecycle of electrical systems — from initial consultation and design through installation, testing, commissioning and ongoing maintenance. We approach every project with a focus on safety, technical correctness and clear communication.

Whether you need a complete new installation, an upgrade of existing systems, solar electrical integration, or responsive fault finding and repairs, we are structured to deliver professional service with integrity.

Our Mission

To deliver safe, reliable and professionally executed electrical installation and engineering solutions that protect people and property, support our clients’ operations, and meet recognised standards of quality and workmanship.

Our Vision

To be a trusted electrical engineering partner in Rwanda — known for technical competence, safety discipline, transparent service and lasting installations that clients can depend on.

Our Values

The principles that guide how we work and how we treat every client and project.

Safety

Electrical safety is non-negotiable. We plan, install and test with the protection of people and assets as a primary objective.

Quality

We specify appropriate materials and apply careful workmanship so that systems perform reliably over the long term.

Integrity

Honest advice, transparent quotations and realistic commitments. We say what we will do and follow through.

Technical Excellence

We stay focused on correct design, proper installation methods and continuous improvement of our technical practice.

Client Focus

We listen carefully, explain options clearly and tailor solutions to the real needs of each client and project.

Reliability

Dependable attendance, professional conduct on site and support after handover are central to how we operate.

Our Professional Approach

How we deliver electrical engineering and installation services from first contact to completed work.

Consultation

We discuss your requirements, site conditions and constraints so that recommendations are practical and aligned with your goals.

Design & Planning

Where required, we support electrical design, load considerations, distribution layout and material selection before work begins.

Installation

Skilled technicians carry out the work with attention to correct methods, neat finish and compliance with good practice.

Testing & Handover

Systems are checked, tested where appropriate, and handed over with clear information so you can operate them confidently.

Commitment to Our Clients

Safety & Quality

We treat safety as a core part of the job — not an afterthought. Proper protective devices, correct earthing, suitable cable sizing and careful installation methods are fundamental to the way we work.

Quality means selecting materials fit for purpose and executing the work so that systems remain serviceable and maintainable for years to come.

Customer Satisfaction

We aim for clear agreements on scope and cost, timely progress updates, and a professional presence on site. When questions arise, we respond constructively.

Long-term relationships matter. We support clients beyond the initial installation with maintenance, inspection and advice when systems need attention.

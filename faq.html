Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about our electrical installation, maintenance and engineering services.

We provide residential, commercial and industrial electrical installation services. This includes new wiring, distribution boards, lighting, power circuits, panel work, solar electrical integration, generator connections and related systems. See our Services page for the full list.
Contact us by phone at +250 788 725 620, email at roi.nipatrick@gmail.com, or use the form on our Contact page. Provide as much detail as possible about the site, scope and any drawings or photos so we can prepare an accurate proposal.
Yes. We handle complete residential electrical installations, upgrades, additional circuits, lighting, distribution board replacement and related work for houses and apartments.
Yes. We provide electrical installation and support for commercial buildings, offices, retail spaces, workshops and industrial facilities. Scope is discussed and agreed project by project based on the requirements and site conditions.
Yes. We offer fault finding, repairs, preventive maintenance and inspection services to keep existing electrical systems safe and operational. Contact us to describe the issue or to arrange a maintenance visit.
Safety is central to our work. We use appropriate materials and protective devices, follow good installation practice, apply safe working procedures on site, and carry out inspection and testing where required. See our Safety & Quality page for more detail.
We provide electrical integration services for solar PV systems, including connection, protection, earthing and interfacing with existing distribution where applicable. Discuss your solar plans with us so we can advise on the electrical side of the installation.
Timelines depend on the size and complexity of the work, site access, material availability and coordination with other trades. After reviewing the scope we provide a realistic programme as part of the quotation. Smaller residential jobs may be completed in days; larger commercial or industrial works take longer and are planned accordingly.
We respond to urgent electrical faults and failures where capacity allows. Contact us by phone for urgent situations. Response times depend on location, nature of the fault and current workload — we will advise honestly when we can attend.
We are based in Rwanda and serve clients nationwide. Travel and logistics for specific locations are discussed when scoping the project.
Yes. We perform visual inspections, functional checks and testing of installations as part of new work and where clients request verification of existing systems. Scope of testing is agreed based on the installation and purpose of the inspection.
